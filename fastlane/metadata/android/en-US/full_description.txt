Soul State is a private, offline, ad-free life operating system designed for Android.

Build discipline, track your money, eat mindfully, and stay focused—all in one application that never sends your personal data to any corporate server.

Key Features:
* Habit Tracker: Build positive habits and break negative ones with streak tracking, frequency options, and completion history.
* To-Do & Repeating Alarms: Task management with custom repeating reminders and reliable notification alarms.
* Expense Tracker: Completely private personal finance tracking with category breakdowns and expense logs.
* Calorie & Meal Log: Mindful nutrition logging with calorie counts and macro tracking.
* Obsidian Markdown Vault: Notes and thoughts saved in standard Markdown format, fully compatible with Obsidian.
* Optional AWS S3 Sync: End-to-end encrypted backup and sync directly to your own personal AWS S3 bucket.
* Privacy & Freedom: 100% offline, zero ads, zero telemetry, zero trackers, and no account required.
